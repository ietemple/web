6An email to the cook

Write to the school cook (60–80 words). Ask for two changes to the menu.

A good email

  1. Greeting: “Dear…,”
  2. Who you are: “I'm a student in 3r ESO…”
  3. Two requests: “Could we have…?” / “We'd like…”
  4. A reason for each request, with because.
  5. A polite closing: “Thank you for…” + “Best wishes,” and your name.

7Check

  • My email has the five parts of the box.
  • It has between 60 and 80 words: words.
  • I used a, an and some correctly.
